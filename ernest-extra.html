11-13 September 2026

CSI - Classroom Science Investigation

ERNEST brings its Classroom Science Investigation format to La Fuori Festival, a three-day public festival dedicated to science, imagination, and contemporary culture. Hosted in an unusual open-air setting in a peripheral area of Rome, the activity follows ERNEST's thread: taking research beyond its usual spaces and turning science into a shared investigation with the people who live the city.

In collaboration with
La Fuori Festival
Location
Piazzale delle Gardenie, Rome
